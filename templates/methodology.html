Data methodology

How the data are structured, cleaned, calculated and what they can and cannot show.

{% if synthetic %}
These data are synthetic. No real-world observation is represented. Every figure on this site is an artefact of the simulation (seed {{ prov.seed if prov else 42 }}) and is useful only to demonstrate the platform. To use real data see “Replacing the synthetic data” below.
{% endif %}

Grain and scope

  • Grain: one row per country × month × fuel type (Petrol, Diesel, LPG).
  • Scope: 84 countries in 7 regions, January 2020 to October 2026 (2026 is a partial year).
  • Record count: the brief asked for 27,468 records. 84 countries × 3 fuels × 82 months = 20,664 complete rows before cleaning; the target is not met and is not claimed. The exact post-cleaning counts are on the data quality page.
  • Prices: nominal USD per litre (LPG per litre-equivalent). No inflation adjustment and no currency conversion is applied; local currency is metadata only.
  • Model: star schema – fact_fuel_price, fact_economy (country × year GDP per capita), dim_country, dim_date, dim_fuel.

Calculations

Tax ratetax ÷ (retail price − tax) × 100 (rate on the pre-tax price)
Tax burdenΣ tax ÷ Σ retail price × 100 (price-weighted “effective” burden)
Subsidy ratioΣ subsidy ÷ Σ retail price × 100
Affordabilityretail price ÷ (GDP per capita ÷ 365) × 100; lower = more affordable
Year-over-yearannual mean price ÷ previous annual mean − 1 (partial years marked *)
VolatilityCV = std ÷ mean of monthly price level; also std of month-over-month % change
Regional meanmean of all monthly observations in the region
CorrelationPearson r across country-level means
Forecastdamped-trend Holt smoothing vs naive baseline; approximate 95% band; 6-month holdout

Cleaning rules

{% if prov %}

The synthetic raw file deliberately contains defects (duplicates, missing values, negative prices, label variants, mixed date formats) so the cleaning logic is exercised. Injected counts are in data/raw/DATA_PROVENANCE.json.

{% endif %}

Exact duplicates are removed; impossible values (non-positive prices, tax rates outside 0–500%) are rejected and logged; missing tax, subsidy and GDP values are filled by documented, field-specific rules and flagged; extreme prices (more than 5× or less than 1/5 of the country-fuel median) are rejected; prices deviating over 35% from a 13-month rolling median are flagged but kept. Every removal is written to data/processed/rejected_rows.csv. The full rule list is on the data quality page.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Income groups use illustrative GDP-per-capita thresholds ({{ cfg.INCOME_BINS[1:4]|join(", ") }} USD), not official World Bank cut-offs.
  • Correlations and scatter plots describe association only; they do not establish that taxes or subsidies cause price differences.
  • Because countries are weighted equally in country-level views, large and small economies count the same.
  • Forecasts are demonstrations on simulated data, not predictions.

Replacing the synthetic data

  1. Prepare a CSV or Excel file at the grain above with columns for country code, country, region, date, fuel type, retail price (USD/L), tax per litre or tax rate, subsidy per litre, and GDP per capita (see docs/data_dictionary.md).
  2. Set RAW_DATA_FILE in .env to the file (and, if its headers differ, edit COLUMN_MAP in config.py).
  3. Run python scripts/run_pipeline.py. The data-origin label switches to “external” automatically and this site’s synthetic banner disappears.
  4. Convert prices to USD (and decide on inflation adjustment) before loading; the pipeline does not do FX conversion.
